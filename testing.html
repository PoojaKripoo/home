10 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A Little More About My Work.

What are your core SEO skills?

My core skills include Technical SEO, On-Page SEO, Off-Page SEO, Local SEO, Keyword Research, Content Strategy and SEO Reporting.

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO focuses on adapting website content and optimisation strategies for search experiences powered by artificial intelligence, alongside traditional SEO practices.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation focuses on improving how content and brand information can be discovered, understood and referenced by generative AI experiences.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimisation focuses on creating clear, useful and structured content that directly answers users' questions.

What is the difference between GEO and AEO?

AEO focuses on providing direct answers to user questions, while GEO focuses on improving content visibility and representation in generative AI experiences.

Which SEO tools do you use?

I work with Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, Ahrefs, SEMrush, Screaming Frog, Moz and SE Ranking.

Do you have team leadership experience?

Yes. My current role includes team mentoring, project coordination and SEO strategy management.

How do you measure SEO performance?

I use organic clicks, impressions, organic sessions, keyword positions and relevant conversions, supported by analytics and reporting tools.
